Enterprise AI agents are software programs that use artificial intelligence to automate tasks, make decisions, and interact with humans. They are designed to perform specific functions, such as data analysis, customer service, or process optimization. As you consider adopting enterprise AI agents, you'll need to think about governance, security, identity and permissions, audit logs, and evaluation.

## Key takeaways
* Governance is crucial for enterprise AI agents
* Security and identity and permissions are essential
* Audit logs help with evaluation and improvement
* Build vs buy decisions depend on specific needs
* Evaluation metrics are critical for success

## What is an Enterprise AI Agent?
An enterprise AI agent is a type of software that uses machine learning and natural language processing to perform tasks, make decisions, and interact with humans. They can be used in various industries, such as healthcare, finance, and customer service. ## Governance and Security
Governance and security are critical components of enterprise AI agents. You'll need to establish clear policies and procedures for data management, access control, and incident response. This includes defining roles and responsibilities, establishing audit logs, and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

### Identity and Permissions
Identity and permissions are essential for ensuring that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive data and systems. You'll need to implement robust authentication and authorization mechanisms, such as multi-factor authentication and role-based access control.

## Evaluation and Metrics
Evaluating the performance of enterprise AI agents is crucial for ensuring their effectiveness and efficiency. You'll need to establish clear metrics and benchmarks, such as accuracy, precision, and recall. This will help you identify areas for improvement and optimize the performance of your AI agents.

| Metric | Description | Importance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Measures the percentage of correct predictions | High |
| Precision | Measures the percentage of true positives | Medium |
| Recall | Measures the percentage of true positives | Medium |
| F1 Score | Measures the balance between precision and recall | High |

## Build vs Buy
When it comes to enterprise AI agents, you'll need to decide whether to build or buy. Building an AI agent from scratch can be time-consuming and costly, but it allows for customization and control. Buying an off-the-shelf AI agent can be faster and more cost-effective, but it may not meet your specific needs.
